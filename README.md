Valence (formerly Shift) is an enterprise AI coaching platform backed by Insight Partners. Its flagship product, Nadia, is an AI leadership coach that helps managers and their teams adopt AI, build skills, and improve teamwork, alongside team tools Align, Habit, Perspective, Reflect 360, and Connect. The platform is enterprise SaaS with SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning, certified SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001, and publishes no public developer API.
