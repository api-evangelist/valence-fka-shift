# Valence (FKA Shift)

> Valence is an enterprise AI coaching platform — its flagship product Nadia is an AI leadership coach that helps managers and their teams adopt AI, build skills, and improve teamwork. Formerly known as Shift, Valence is backed by Insight Partners. The platform also includes team tools: Align, Habit, Perspective, Reflect 360, and Connect. Valence publishes no public developer API; access is via the coach.valence.co application with enterprise SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning.
